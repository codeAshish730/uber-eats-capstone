DAY 5: HOURLY DEMAND FORECASTING — BUSINESS INTERPRETATION

1. OBJECTIVE

The objective was to analyze hourly Uber Eats order demand, identify time-based patterns, and evaluate forecasting models to support operational planning.

2. DATASET FINDINGS

- Total orders: 1,000
- Available hourly observations: 1
- Observed hour: October 23, 2026, 3:00 PM
- Orders in that hour: 1,000

All orders fall within the same hourly period in the available dataset. This limits the analysis of demand over time.

3. TIME-SERIES ANALYSIS

Hourly demand aggregation and visualization were completed.

Trend, seasonality, and residual decomposition could not be performed meaningfully because only one hourly observation is available.

The Augmented Dickey-Fuller (ADF) stationarity test was not performed because the dataset is insufficient for the test.

4. FORECASTING MODELS

ARIMA and Prophet were considered, but neither model was trained because sufficient historical hourly observations were unavailable.

A chronological training and test split could not be created reliably. Consequently, MAE, RMSE, and MAPE are unavailable, and no model performance ranking can be reported.

5. BUSINESS IMPLICATIONS

The current dataset cannot reliably identify peak ordering hours, weekday or weekend patterns, or future demand.

Uber Eats should avoid using this dataset alone to schedule drivers, allocate restaurant capacity, or predict future order volumes.

For a meaningful demand forecasting system, collect order timestamps across multiple weeks or months, including weekdays, weekends, holidays, and different times of day.

With sufficient historical data, the team can evaluate ARIMA and Prophet using chronological validation and compare MAE, RMSE, and MAPE.

6. CONCLUSION

The Day 5 data preparation, exploratory checks, model feasibility checks, and documentation have been completed.

The forecasting objective remains limited by the available data. Reliable forecasts and model comparisons require a larger historical dataset with observations across many hours and days.

The results have been documented transparently without fabricating forecasts or evaluation metrics.
