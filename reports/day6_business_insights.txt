DAY 6: UBER EATS BUSINESS INSIGHTS AND RECOMMENDATIONS

1. CUSTOMER SEGMENTATION

Customer Cluster 0 averages 7.68 orders and 427.34 in total spending per customer. Cluster 1 averages 3.74 orders and 209.74 in spending.

Recommendation: Prioritize retention initiatives for Cluster 0. Test targeted promotions for Cluster 1 to encourage repeat orders. Measure results before expanding campaigns.

Limitation: The customer segments are based on synthetic data, and weekend and late-night order counts are zero.

2. RESTAURANT SEGMENTATION

Restaurant Cluster 0 contains 20 restaurants and averages 35.65 orders, 2012.37 in revenue, a rating of 2.96, a cancellation rate of 0.35, and a delivery time of 43.76 minutes.

Cluster 1 contains 10 restaurants and averages 28.70 orders, 1563.42 in revenue, a rating of 3.13, a cancellation rate of 0.34, and a delivery time of 40.59 minutes.

Recommendation: Review cancellations, food quality, and fulfillment processes in Cluster 0 first. Track cancellation rates, customer ratings, and delivery times to assess improvement.

3. CUSTOMER SENTIMENT

The sentiment model achieved 37% test accuracy. Review text includes references to cold food and late delivery, but repeated synthetic reviews sometimes have conflicting ratings.

Recommendation: Investigate cold-food complaints and late deliveries using verified customer feedback. Improve the review dataset before using automated sentiment predictions for operational decisions.

Limitation: The current sentiment model is not reliable enough for automated high-impact decisions.

4. DELIVERY-TIME PREDICTION

Ridge Regression achieved a test MAE of 14.792 minutes, RMSE of 16.799 minutes, and R-squared of -0.013. Random Forest achieved a test MAE of 14.930 minutes and R-squared of -0.055.

Recommendation: Improve the model using more informative data, such as actual route distance, traffic, weather, restaurant preparation time, and pickup waiting time. Evaluate improvements against a baseline on held-out data.

Limitation: Current features have very weak correlations with delivery time. Feature importance does not establish causation.

5. TIP PREDICTION

Tip prediction has not been implemented because a suitable tip amount target has not been identified in the available project data.

Recommendation: Obtain reliable tip amount data linked to orders before building and evaluating a tip prediction model.

6. DEMAND FORECASTING

The hourly demand dataset contains 1,000 orders aggregated into a single hour on October 23, 2026. ARIMA and Prophet were not trained, and no forecast accuracy metrics are available.

Recommendation: Collect order history across multiple weeks or months. Then evaluate forecasts using chronological validation and MAE, RMSE, and MAPE before planning rider capacity by hour or location.

7. OVERALL CONCLUSION

The integrated pipeline successfully executes the existing feature engineering, segmentation, sentiment, delivery evaluation, and demand feasibility scripts.

The current results support preliminary operational investigations, not fully reliable automated decisions. Synthetic data, limited temporal coverage, weak predictive performance, and missing tip data remain important limitations.

Future work should prioritize better historical data, stronger model validation, reproducible runs, and monitoring of business outcomes after recommendations are implemented.
